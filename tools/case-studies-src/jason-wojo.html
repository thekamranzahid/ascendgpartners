Client Overview

Jason Wojo is the founder of Wojo Media, one of the fastest-growing digital marketing firms in the United States and an Inc. 5000 honoree. Wojo Media runs high-volume coaching and event campaigns, processing tens of thousands of leads across multiple simultaneous pipelines.

Jason came to Ascend Growth Partners with two distinct but connected challenges: his personal brand was not keeping pace with his business results, and his sales pipeline could not scale without burning out his team.

We solved both.

Part 1: PR and Media Authority

The Challenge

Jason had built Wojo Media into a nationally recognized firm, but his personal brand told a different story. A search for his name returned thin results that did not reflect the caliber of his work or the scale of his company. Against competitors crowding the digital marketing space, his social proof was underwhelming.

High-value opportunities were slipping away. Speaking invitations, podcast bookings, and inbound partnership offers were going to names with stronger media footprints. Jason's expertise was not in question. His visibility was.

His goal was clear: build the kind of media authority that matched his results and unlocked bigger doors.

Our Strategy

Our approach to Jason's press and PR campaign was built on layered media placements and strategic sequencing. We did not chase logos for a press page. We built a deliberate architecture of coverage designed to dominate search results, fuel sales conversations, and open doors to podcasts and stages.

We started by identifying the outlets that would land hardest for Jason's positioning as a data-driven marketing leader. Early in the campaign, we discovered that tying his personal story directly to Wojo Media's Inc. 5000 recognition resonated far more than generic founder profiles. That insight shaped everything that followed.

From there, we built outward across tiers, moving from anchor features in business publications to national list placements to broadcast television. Each placement was designed to compound the last.

What We Secured

We anchored the campaign with substantive features in Flaunt Magazine, Tech Times, and Entrepreneur, tying Jason's leadership directly to Wojo Media's Inc. 5000 win.

We secured "Top Entrepreneur" list placements across E! News, NY Magazine, Daily Caller, MSN, and LA Weekly, creating a multi-year pattern of recognition that signals sustained authority rather than a one-time mention.

We layered in national broadcast television with segments on Tampa Bay's Morning Blend and Inside South Florida, adding on-camera credibility and shareable video assets that Jason's team could deploy across sales and marketing channels.

The Results

Google page one for Jason's name. Tier-1 outlet features now dominate search results for "Jason Wojo," turning prospect research into pre-sold sales calls before he ever picks up the phone.

Steady increase in podcast bookings. As Jason's press footprint grew, hosts and booking teams began pitching him for higher-tier shows rather than the other way around.

Inbound speaking and partnership opportunities. Event organizers and podcast hosts began reaching out directly, shifting Jason from chasing opportunities to fielding them.

The compounding effect of stacked tier-1 features, combined with broadcast TV and consistent national recognition, turned Jason's visibility gap into a long-term authority asset. The press footprint now does the selling before he gets on a call.

Part 2: Voice AI and Pipeline Automation

The Challenge

Wojo Media processes tens of thousands of leads across multiple coaching and event campaigns simultaneously. Calling, qualifying, and booking each lead manually was never viable at this scale. They needed a system that could run the entire pipeline from first dial to confirmed appointment, across three distinct campaign types, without a single human making a phone call.

Our Solution

Ascend deployed a custom AI solutions build for Wojo Media, an AI voice agent named Sophia.

Sophia runs three simultaneous campaign types at once. She handles SKOOL advisor bookings with full qualification on business details and ad readiness. She manages SYA event opt-in detection with automatic ticket link delivery via SMS. She runs direct appointment scheduling through Calendly. Sophia draws from a unified knowledge base spanning all three campaign contexts, keeping qualification precise regardless of which campaign a lead entered through.

Booking works through agentic tool calling. Mid-conversation, Sophia calls a custom tool that navigates Calendly in a real browser, filling form fields and confirming time slots in real time without any human involvement.

At 150,641 calls placed, voicemail detection is critical to protecting dial economics. Sophia identifies voicemails within seconds and either drops a clean pre-recorded message or disconnects instantly, keeping throughput high and cost-per-contact low.

After every call, the full transcript is analyzed to detect event attendance intent and trigger automated follow-ups. Every follow-up across voice, SMS, and email carries complete cross-channel context from every previous interaction. No lead ever hears the same message twice.

Automated test suites validated every flow before launch and run continuous regression testing to maintain quality across the full volume of dials.

Technical Highlights

  • Agentic tool calling books appointments through Calendly via real browser automation mid-conversation
  • Transcript analysis detects event opt-ins and fires tool actions to send ticket links via SMS automatically
  • 99%+ voicemail detection across 150,000+ calls with clean message drops to protect outbound throughput
  • Cross-channel memory across every SMS and email follow-up referencing full voice, text, and email history
  • Local presence dialing across 300+ area codes with SHAKEN/STIR attestation and double-dial technology
  • Sub-650ms voice latency with barge-in, endpointing, and natural turn-taking
