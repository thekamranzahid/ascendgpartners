How fast does Google Ads start working?+

You'll see early signal — clicks, impressions, initial conversions — within the first 1–2 weeks. Stable, optimizable performance typically takes 4–6 weeks of data collection, testing, and bid algorithm learning. Anyone promising "results in 7 days" is either lying or burning your budget on broad match to fake the curve. We'd rather build something that compounds than spike a vanity number in week one.

Does Ascend manage Shopping and Performance Max campaigns?+

Yes — Ascend manages the full Google Ads stack: Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Demand Gen, Discovery, and remarketing. Performance Max in particular has become non-negotiable for most ecommerce accounts, but it's also the campaign type most agencies run badly because the asset group structure is unforgiving. We're built for it.

Can you fix a Google Ads account that's wasting spend?+

Yes — and account remediation is one of the most common ways clients start working with us. We audit account structure first, then conversion tracking, then search terms, then bidding strategy, then landing pages, then creative. Most "broken" accounts have 2–3 leaks doing the bulk of the damage, not 30 small ones — finding and patching those quickly is usually where the first ROAS lift comes from.

What's the minimum ad spend Ascend works with?+

Our Google Ads engagements are typically a fit for brands spending $10K/month and up on paid media — that's the spend level where the work we do (server-side tracking, value-based bidding, dedicated optimization cycles) actually pays for itself. Below that, the management fee outweighs the upside, and we'll usually point smaller accounts toward DIY resources or different solutions until they're ready.

Do you work with personal brands, course creators, and authors — or only ecommerce?+

Both, and they're different playbooks. Course launches, book launches, and high-ticket personal-brand offers run on a launch-cycle model with very different bidding strategies than evergreen ecommerce. Ascend has run paid media for some of the largest personal brands in the world alongside our ecommerce clients, and we don't try to force one playbook onto the other.

How does Google Ads fit with Ascend's other services?+

Tightly. Paid ads at Ascend run alongside SEO, PR, content, and email — which means our Google Ads clients get audience signals (PR-driven brand searches), landing page support (web design), and lifecycle capture (email and SMS) that pure-PPC agencies can't deliver. Most of our paid clients started with one service and expanded inside six months because the channels compound when they talk to each other.

What's the difference between hiring Ascend vs. a typical PPC agency?+

Three things. One: we lead with measurement — server-side tracking, offline conversions, and revenue-based bidding before we touch a campaign. Two: we run paid as part of an integrated stack with PR, SEO, content, and email, so the channels reinforce each other. Three: we work specifically with founders, personal brands, and challenger companies, so our playbooks are tuned for direct-response and brand-led work — not enterprise lead-gen funnels nobody buys from anyway.
