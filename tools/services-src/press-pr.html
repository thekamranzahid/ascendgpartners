Step 01 — Brand Audit

Before we pitch a single journalist, we read the room.

Most PR engagements fail in week one — not because of bad pitching, but because no one mapped the story landscape first. We audit your current media footprint, public perception, search results, AI citations, and competitor narrative share. We find the stories already being told about you (the good and the ugly), the stories that should be told, and the open lanes where your brand can own the conversation. That map becomes the entire campaign brief.

Step 02 — Comms Readiness

Most brands break the moment the press shows up.

Spokespeople who haven't been media-trained. Press kits that don't exist. Messaging that contradicts itself across channels. Founder bios that are three years out of date. We rebuild the communications infrastructure before we go to market — press kits, founder positioning, message hierarchy, response protocols, and crisis playbooks — so when the story lands, your brand is ready to capitalize on it instead of fumble it.

Step 03 — Story & Pitching

Journalists don't write about products. They write about stories.

This is where most PR firms quietly fail — they send press releases pretending to be stories. We engineer actual narrative angles: contrarian takes journalists are itching to publish, data-backed trends with original research behind them, founder origin stories with real stakes, and cultural moments your brand has earned the right to be part of. Then we pitch them through relationships, not blast lists. Our team places clients in Forbes, WSJ, Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Inc., Fast Company, Business Insider, and the verticals that matter for your category.

Step 03 — *Story & Pitching*

Step 04 — Engineered Opportunity

The best PR moments aren't waited for. They're built.

Reactive PR - waiting for press to come find you - is a losing game. We forecast upcoming news cycles, industry inflection points, awards seasons, and cultural conversations where your brand can insert itself credibly. Then we build the moments: original research drops, founder bylines, expert commentary at the right time, partnership announcements, and tied-to-the-news Op-Eds.

How long does it take to land Tier 1 media coverage?+

Most Ascend clients see their first Tier 1 placement within 60–90 days. The first month is spent on brand audit, narrative development, comms readiness, and quietly warming the right journalist relationships — going to market on day one with a half-built story is how PR campaigns die. Once we're pitching, placements compound from there.

What counts as a Tier 1 media placement?+

Tier 1 placements are earned editorial coverage in major national and international outlets: Forbes, The Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, Reuters, Business Insider, Fast Company, TechCrunch, Inc., and the leading vertical publications in your industry. Sponsored content, paid placements, and contributor pieces in low-authority outlets don't count — and any agency telling you they do is the wrong agency.

Does Ascend guarantee placements?+

No ethical PR firm guarantees specific placements — and any firm that does is either lying or paying for them. What Ascend guarantees is process: a real audit, a real story, the right journalist relationships, the right moments to pitch into, and the persistence to land coverage that actually moves the needle. The proof is the client roster.

How is PR different from paid advertising or influencer marketing?+

Paid ads disappear the moment the budget stops. Earned media is permanent — it gets indexed by Google, cited by ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, and carries third-party editorial credibility that no ad can replicate. Influencer marketing rents a creator's audience; PR builds your own. The brands that win the long game invest in all three, but PR is the only one that compounds.

Can Ascend handle crisis communications?+

We build proactive crisis playbooks for brands before they need them, and we provide rapid-response support when something breaks. The brands we work with — public-facing personalities, combat sports, consumer brands with active social audiences — operate in environments where reputational risk is constant, so we're built for it.
