What email and SMS platforms does Ascend work with?+

Ascend works across every major email and SMS platform: Klaviyo, Attentive, Postscript, HubSpot, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Drip, ConvertKit, Beehiiv, and Substack. We'll recommend the right platform for your stack — ecommerce brands usually land on Klaviyo + Attentive, while creators and personal brands often run Beehiiv, ConvertKit, or HubSpot. We manage migrations, setup, and ongoing operations end-to-end.

How fast can you build a full email automation system?+

A complete core automation suite — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back, browse abandonment — typically takes 3–4 weeks from kickoff to live. We prioritize highest-revenue flows first, so you usually see meaningful return inside the first month rather than waiting for the full build to finish.

How does Ascend measure email and SMS success?+

Revenue per send, attributed email and SMS revenue, flow conversion rates, list growth rate, and deliverability health. We don't lead reports with open rates — opens stopped being a reliable metric the moment Apple Mail Privacy Protection launched. Every dashboard ties campaign and flow performance back to actual dollars.

Can SMS marketing actually drive meaningful revenue?+

Yes — SMS regularly delivers 95%+ open rates and click-through rates several multiples higher than email. The catch is that SMS punishes overuse hard: subscribers opt out fast if abused. We deploy SMS surgically — for cart recovery, drops, time-sensitive offers, and VIP segments — which is what unlocks the revenue without burning the list.

What if our email list is small, or hasn't been emailed in a long time?+

Both are normal starting points. For small lists, we build a list-growth strategy alongside the program — popups, lead magnets, partnership flows, paid acquisition where it pencils out. For dormant lists, we run a careful sunset and re-engagement sequence before any major sends so we protect your sender reputation. Sending hard to a cold list is the fastest way to land in spam permanently.

Do you work with personal brands and creators, or only ecommerce?+

Both — and they're completely different playbooks. Ecommerce email is product-driven, transactional, and lives in Klaviyo or HubSpot. Creator email is editorial, audience-driven, and lives in Beehiiv, ConvertKit, or Substack. Ascend has run programs for some of the largest personal brands in the world alongside our ecommerce work, and we don't try to force one playbook onto the other.

How does email and SMS fit with the rest of Ascend's services?+

Email and SMS sit at the bottom of the funnel — they capture and monetize the demand that PR, SEO, social, and paid generate at the top. Ascend clients running our integrated stack typically see email and SMS revenue grow 2–3x faster than clients running standalone programs, because the audiences feeding the list are higher-intent to begin with. The channels compound when they talk to each other.
