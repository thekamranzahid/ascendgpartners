How long does SEO take to actually pay off?+

Most Ascend clients see measurable ranking and traffic gains within 3–6 months, with revenue impact compounding from month 6 onward. SEO is the only marketing channel where last year's work keeps paying this year — but it punishes shortcuts. We optimize for compounding, not for vanity wins in month one.

How is Ascend Growth's SEO approach different from other agencies?+

Most SEO agencies do SEO. Ascend Growth Partners does PR, content, and SEO as one integrated system — which means our SEO clients get the link authority that pure-SEO agencies can't reach. Our team has run search and PR strategy for clients including Mel Robbins, Ed Mylett, Tim Grover, Gymshark, and BKFC.

Do you handle SEO for personal brands and individual founders, or only companies?+

Both — and personal-brand SEO is one of our specialties. We work with thought leaders, authors, athletes, and founders who need to own the search results around their own name, their book, their podcast, or their movement. The playbook is different from corporate SEO, and we've run it for some of the biggest personal brands in the world.

What does your reporting actually show me?+

A live dashboard with keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversion attribution, backlinks earned, and competitive benchmarking — plus a monthly strategy call where we walk through what shipped, what moved, and what's next. No PDFs nobody reads.

Can you recover a site that got hit by a Google algorithm update?+

Yes — algorithm recovery and helpful-content remediation is one of Ascend's core specialties. How does SEO fit with PR, AI, and your other services?+

Most agencies sell SEO, PR, and AI as separate line items.
